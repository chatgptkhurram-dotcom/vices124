Controls
RPG: K = character & skills (or tap the purple XP bar). Walk up to a house gate and press E / ENTER to buy it, then go inside: sleep, safe, gun locker, upgrades.
PC: WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, click screen to capture mouse (mouse = look, left click = FIRE, or hold F), E enter / exit car, M big map, Q weapon menu (buy guns here), T send money to friends, P pause, R radio (in a car), walk into the blue ATM / yellow GARAGE / pink STYLE SHOP ( skin, hair, clothes, shoes, hat, shades) / white HOSPITAL markers. If you get wasted you wake up at a hospital (bill $200). A day lasts 6 minutes - the clock is next to your health bar, keys 1-6 pick owned gun. Crash any car 3 times and it EXPLODES! Fire trucks rush to put out the fire. Police cars come from police stations. Drive a car into the orange GARAGE to paint / tune / armor it. Press G (or HONK) in a car for a funny horn. Shoot near people and they scream and run, some dance, some wear clown wigs. In a police car, ambulance or fire truck press H (or the SIREN button) for the siren.
Mobile: left stick move, drag screen to look, FIRE / JUMP / ENTER / MAP / GUNS buttons, SEND MONEY button on the left.
Follow the yellow marker to start a mission. Green marker = gun shop. Hurt people and police chase you.
Aircraft (AIRPORT on the east beach, cyan beam)
Walk to a plane/heli, press E / ENTER. Heli: W/S fly, A/D turn, Space/▲ up, Shift/▼ down. Plane: W/S throttle, A/D turn, Space/▲ climb, Shift/▼ dive. Take off at 35+ m/s, land gently, then E to exit.
Neon graphics (glow, sunset, underglow)
